# Loop

A Loyalty, Reward & Product Experimentation platform that should store reliable records of customer purchases, accrued loyalty credit, and reward redemptions.
The platform must prevent duplicate awards and overspending, while later providing events and metrics that accurately reflect the underlying transactions.

Users include:
(1) A customer who wants to earn and redeem points; 
(2) a business operator who wants a clear view of programme activity and eventually wants to test product changes.

Inital Architecture
One Spring Boot deployable, one PostgreSQL instance, two simple web interfaces
